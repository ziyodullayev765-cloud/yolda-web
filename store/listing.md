```
• Разместите груз за минуту: откуда куда, что, сколько килограммов.

• Смотрите открытые грузы с фильтрами по направлению, весу и типу машины.

• Продайте свою машину: фото, год, пробег, цена и технические данные.
• Сохраняйте понравившиеся, звоните продавцу напрямую.
```

### Full description

```
YO'LDA is a freight marketplace for Uzbekistan. Shippers and drivers meet in one place — no middlemen, no endless phone calls.

FOR SHIPPERS

• Post a load in a minute: where from, where to, what, how many kilograms.
• The app works out the price itself, from distance and weight.
• Drivers send you offers with a price and an arrival time.
• You compare the offers and choose the driver YOURSELF. Not whoever taps first.
• Once you have chosen, the phone numbers open up and you talk directly.
• You can see which stage the load is at: heading to pickup, loaded, on the way, delivered.
• If a driver stops answering, release them and the load goes back to the open list.

FOR DRIVERS

• Browse open loads, filtered by route, weight and vehicle type.
• Save a search and get a notification when a matching load appears.
• Send an offer with your own price.
• In "My load", the job you took fits on one screen: route, price, cargo details, the next-stage button and the shipper's number.
• One button at each stage — that is how the shipper sees where the load is.
• If your plans change you can give the load up, and it returns to the open list.
• Backhaul: loads leaving the city you have just delivered to are shown separately, so you do not drive back empty.

VEHICLE MARKETPLACE

• Listings for trucks, vans, trailers, tippers, refrigerated trucks and special equipment.
• Sell your own vehicle: photos, year, mileage, price and technical details.
• Save the ones you like and call the seller directly.

TRUST

• Users who have verified their documents carry a badge.
• After delivery both sides rate each other, and the rating stays on the profile.
• A profile shows the number of loads delivered and the reviews left.

PRICE AND PAYMENT

The app is completely free for now: signing up, posting a load and sending offers carry no commission. YO'LDA does not take payments and does not hold money — you settle with the driver directly.

LANGUAGES

Uzbek, Russian and English.

PRIVACY

Your location (GPS) is never requested or collected. There are no ad networks, trackers or analytics. Your phone number is not shown on a load listing — it is revealed only after you have chosen a driver, and only to those two parties.
```
